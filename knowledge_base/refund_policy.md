# AgentFlow Refund and Dispute Policy

## 1. Overview
This document specifies the refund guidelines, financial dispute protocols, and eligibility criteria for customer support and automated ticket processing.

## 2. Refund Eligibility Criteria
- **Failed Payment / Multiple Charges**: If a customer is charged multiple times for a single transaction or if money was deducted after a failed transaction status, a 100% full refund is issued automatically upon verification.
- **Service Outage**: If services are unavailable for more than 48 continuous hours, customers are eligible for a prorated monthly credit or full refund.
- **14-Day Money Back Guarantee**: New subscribers may request a full refund within 14 days of original purchase if dissatisfied with the product.

## 3. High-Risk Financial Dispute Protocols
- Any refund request exceeding **$100.00** or involving bank chargebacks / fraud claims is classified as **HIGH RISK**.
- All High-Risk financial transactions REQUIRE **Human Approval** by a Finance / Billing Manager before funds are disbursed.
- Automated agents MUST NOT process refunds directly when potential fraud or duplicate billing disputes exceed threshold limits.

## 4. Processing Timelines
- Approved credit card refunds take **3 to 5 business days** to reflect in the customer's bank account.
- Direct bank transfer refunds take **5 to 7 business days**.
